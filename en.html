Wearfolio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Türkçe

Wearfolio is a wardrobe assistant that organizes your clothes and suggests outfits based on the weather and your plans. This policy explains what data is kept where and what leaves your device.

In short

  • There are no accounts, and we run no server or cloud database.
  • Your wardrobe, photos, plans and settings stay only in the app’s storage on your phone.
  • We do not use AI; none of your photos or details are sent to an AI service.
  • We use no ads, tracking or analytics tools, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Data kept on your device

Photos and details of your pieces (color, type, formality, brand, price…), your wear history, plans, Style DNA and preferences, personal color quiz answers and app settings are stored on your phone. This data does not reach us. Deleting the app removes it from your phone, and you can erase everything at any time with “Delete all my data” under Ben → Gizlilik (Privacy).

Data that leaves your device

To whomWhat is sentWhen
Open-Meteo (weather) Coordinates of the city you picked or of your device location. When showing the weather.
Shopping sites The in-app browser loads only pages you open. When you tap “select this product”, the product image and price are read from the page. When you open a site.
Backup file You create the backup and decide where to save it (Files, Drive, email…). It is not encrypted; keep it somewhere you trust. When you tap “Back up”.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph clothes and products.
  • Photos: only the photos you select are accessed.
  • Location (while using the app): approximate location for the weather; it is not stored.
  • Calendar (read): to suggest outfits for your events, only when you start an import.
  • Notifications: evening reminders are scheduled locally on your phone; nothing is pushed from a server.

All permissions are optional. If you decline, the related feature is turned off and the app keeps working. You can withdraw permissions in your phone’s settings at any time.

Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you can access, correct and delete it directly in the app. We hold no data about you that we could delete on request. You can write to the address below with questions or requests.

Children

Wearfolio is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear on this page. If the use of your data changes significantly, you will also be informed in the app.
